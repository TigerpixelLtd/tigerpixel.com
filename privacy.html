Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Tigerpixel Ltd (“Tigerpixel”, “we”, “us”) handles personal data in connection with our mobile apps and this website. Tigerpixel Ltd is a company registered in the United Kingdom.

Information our apps collect

Our apps do not collect, store or share any personal data. They do not require an account, and they do not use analytics, advertising or tracking services. Any information you enter into an app stays on your device.

App stores

When you download an app from Google Play or another app store, the store operator may collect information about you under its own privacy policy. We do not control that collection. For Google Play, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses for security and operational purposes. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you contact us

If you email us, we will receive your email address and anything you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and keep it only for as long as needed for that purpose. We do not sell or share it.

Children

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and revise the date above.
